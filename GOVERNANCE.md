# Governance

## Roles

- **Users** use OpenLabel and report issues.
- **Contributors** submit pull requests, reviews, documentation or tickets.
- **Maintainers** (listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)) review and merge pull requests, triage tickets and cut releases.

## Decisions

- Day-to-day decisions are made in pull requests and tickets by lazy consensus: a proposal stands if no maintainer objects within a reasonable time.
- Significant technical decisions are written as Architecture Decision Records in [docs/adr](docs/adr) and need approval from at least one maintainer who did not write them.
- If consensus can't be reached, maintainers vote; a simple majority decides.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors with a sustained record of quality contributions and reviews can be nominated by an existing maintainer and are added after approval by a majority of maintainers.

## Code of conduct

All participants follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
